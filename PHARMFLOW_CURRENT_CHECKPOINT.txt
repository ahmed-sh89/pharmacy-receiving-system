before opening Dashboard, while the stale flash itself was not solved.

- valid Sign In opens Dashboard immediately;
- cloud/server workspace boot continues in the background;
- authentication/access checks remain unchanged;
- no Receiving, Finalize, Archive, Expiry, Global GTIN, database or sync
  business logic is modified.

## EXPECTED
After pressing Sign In, Dashboard opens promptly again instead of waiting for
the full cloud hydration cycle.

## KNOWN SEPARATE ISSUE
The brief stale Dashboard flash is NOT claimed fixed by this release.
It remains a separate deferred issue and must not be confused with Sign-In speed.

## DIAGNOSTIC UPDATE
The flash persisted even after Reset Current Order.
This disproved the remaining-workspace-persistence explanation for the visible
frame itself.

## FULL SOURCE TRACE — CONFIRMED ROOT CAUSE
Sign-in flow was:

1. authenticate;
2. loadMyAppContext();
3. renderAuthState();
4. renderAuthState() saw valid application access and immediately executed:
      authGate.classList.remove("visible")
5. only after that, unlockApplicationAfterAuth() started/continued the async
   server-authoritative workspace hydration;
6. the already-mounted Dashboard DOM was exposed between steps 4 and 5/6.

Therefore the flash could survive even when Current Workspace was reset.
It was a UI reveal race in auth.js.

2C.11.4.9 attempted to keep the gate until hydration, but renderAuthState()
was independently removing the gate first.

## ROOT FIX
- renderAuthState() no longer hides authGate for an authenticated user.
- unlockApplicationAfterAuth() is the ONLY reveal authority.
- It already waits for server-authoritative hydration, then removes authGate.
- No state, persistence, Receiving, Finalize, Archive, Expiry, GTIN or database
  logic changed.

5. Dashboard must appear once in its final authoritative state.
